### 11.5.2 CRUD and Query Operations

Once an UDO is created, you can treat it as an ordinary entity. The URL for UDO CRUD and Query operation are the same as the internal SAP Business One entities.

**Creating UDO Entity**

> **Sample Code**
>
> <!-- code: c239-03 -->
> ```http
> POST /MyOrders
> {
>     "U_CustomerName": "c1",
>     "U_DocTotal": 620,

>             "U_ItemName": "item1",

>             "U_Quantity": 3
>         },
>         {
>             "U_ItemName": "item2",
