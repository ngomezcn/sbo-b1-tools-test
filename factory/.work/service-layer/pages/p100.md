## 3.16 Attachments

As of SAP Business One 9.1 patch level 12, version for SAP HANA, attachment manipulation is supported through the Service Layer. The supported attachment type list is:

- pdf
- doc
- docx
- jpg
- jpeg
- png
- txt
- xls
- ppt
